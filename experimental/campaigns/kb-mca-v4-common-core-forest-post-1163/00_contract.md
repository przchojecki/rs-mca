# Epistemic Contract

## Target

For the deployed KoalaBear MCA row

```text
(n,k,m,B*)=(2097152,1048576,1116048,274980728111395087),
```

construct a source-bound whole-line compiler for actual degree-`<k`
explanation records in the active Grande Finale v4 S/A/E chronology.  Every
distinct finite affine bad slope on one received line must be assigned exactly
once to an earlier owner, a fixed-core staircase family, a shortened
direction residual, an `s>=14` common-core residual, or
`UNPAID_PRIMITIVE`.  The assignment must preserve the same line, slope,
support, explanation, owner, and chronology.

The fixed-core payment is usable only through an aggregate line-level theorem;
summing a per-core bound over realized cores is forbidden.

## Mandatory guards

- Use actual degree-`<k` explanations, or require `s_k(W,N)<=omega` when a
  `K=k+1` shifted-lattice representation is used.
- Charge the near-rational owner by `2w=134944`, separately from the
  31-slope exception reserve.
- Reject the #1160 line from the BC/common-core residual.
- Canonicalize complete realizable records, never numerical profiles.

## Acceptable adjacent outputs

- A chronology-correct aggregate common-core forest theorem with exact fibers.
- A same-owner maximum-type theorem bypassing the forest.
- An actual guarded collision showing the first proposed ownership rule is
  false, with the weakest repair theorem isolated.
- A proved primitive residual with frozen equations and a falsifier.

## Evidence standard

`candidate` requires exact source pins, a proof, executable normal and hostile
controls, and exact integer arithmetic. `provisionally-verified` additionally
requires an independent implementation (Sage/FLINT/Wolfram as appropriate).
`handoff` requires an isolated mathematical reviewer distinct from the
generator. Numerical agreement alone is evidence, not proof.

## Prohibited overclaims

- A deterministic selector without an aggregate fiber/add-back bound is not a
  payment.
- A local record core is not presumed to be a slope invariant.
- A toy collision is not a deployed-row counterexample.
- The `2w` owner is not part of the 31-slope reserve.
- No local packet closes KoalaBear without the complete v4 chronology.

## Stop and handoff conditions

Stop a proposed bridge at its first false implication and preserve the exact
counterexample.  Hand a stabilized theorem/certificate to an isolated source,
mathematics, and custody review before marking it bankable.
