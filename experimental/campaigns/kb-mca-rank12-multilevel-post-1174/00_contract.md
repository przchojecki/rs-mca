## Evidence standard

Candidate status requires a complete statement, exact integer optimizer,
hostile controls, and all dependencies pinned to source.  Provisional
verification requires independent mathematics and certificate reviews,
positive and negative controls, normal/optimized replay, and mutations.
Handoff additionally requires active-source integration, a complete review
status table, and an exact effect on the declared post-near branch.

## Prohibited overclaims

- Numerical agreement is not proof.
- An adjacent theorem is not evidence for the original conjecture without a proved implication.
- The single-cutoff wall is not a lower bound on an actual rank-twelve family.
- Old descendants or four-block payments are not reusable until their
  source-bound implications survive on exact #1174 ancestry.
- No local rank-twelve result moves an active-v4 atom without a separately
  proved chronology adapter.

## Stop and handoff conditions

Stop a mechanism when an actual-record control falsifies its first bridge or
an exact optimizer proves its whole inequality class cannot fit \(E\).  Hand
off only after independent theorem/certificate review.  Lean is deferred
until the local coupling theorem is stable.
