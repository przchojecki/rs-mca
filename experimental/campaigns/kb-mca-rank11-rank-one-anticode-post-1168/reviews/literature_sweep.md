# Targeted literature sweep

Date: 2026-08-15

Tool: Exa targeted search for primary or standard sources on bilinear-forms
graphs, rank-metric anticodes, and cliques of matrices at pairwise rank
distance one.

Sources inspected included Delsarte's 1978 paper on bilinear forms over
finite fields, standard bilinear-forms-graph treatments, and later matrix
graph papers recording the two maximal-clique geometries (fixed image and
fixed kernel/row type). This is the correct external context for the packet.

No external theorem is load-bearing. For the special \(2\times s\) case
needed here, the packet gives a direct two-line-factor proof valid over an
arbitrary field. The Reed--Solomon core-overlap implication and both owner
projections are likewise proved internally.

The search did not locate a theorem that forces a large rank-one clique
inside the actual rank-eleven minimizing-pair family, nor a
chronology-preserving decomposition into few such cliques. That remains the
next aggregate joint.
