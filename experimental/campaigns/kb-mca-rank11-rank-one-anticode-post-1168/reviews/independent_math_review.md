## 1. Matrix classification

Translate one coefficient matrix to zero. Every remaining matrix has rank at
most one. Fix a nonzero member \(uv^T\). For another member \(xy^T\),
the \(2\times s\) matrix \(xy^T-uv^T\) has rank at most one only if either
\(x\parallel u\) or \(y\parallel v\). If a strict fixed-left member and a
strict fixed-right member both occur, their difference has rank two. Hence
the entire family shares a left factor or shares a right factor. The
argument is characteristic-free and does not require maximality.
