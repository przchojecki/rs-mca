# Controls

## Positive controls

1. **Complete domains.** Every shortening uses the complete scalar agreement
   domain and lifts simultaneous pair explanations by the locator.
2. **Refrozen minimizers.** Minimizing directions and pair cores are selected
   again after every shortening.
3. **Uniform rank-one base.** The residual dimension `j=K-u`, not the original
   ambient dimension, is scanned through the full deployed range.
4. **Vertical coordinates retained.** Zeros of the direction polynomial that
   are not universal become vertical projective lines; they are not discarded.
5. **Distinct pair types.** Two complete pair cores intersect in at most
   `K-1` coordinates.
6. **Disjoint fixed-pair exceptions.** Outside one complete pair core, one
   coordinate determines at most one finite slope.
7. **Strict barrier ordering.** `b_s=b_(s-1)+1`, so a proper drop above one
   barrier enters the next stage above its barrier.
8. **No nested-load sum.** Only one source-bound descendant is followed.

## Hostile controls

1. The old endpoint-only induction is rejected.
2. The rank-twelve route is not promoted to a payment.
3. The `279,911` value is a capacity excess, not a constructed extremizer.
4. Pairwise Möbius/subgroup incidence bounds are not imported for arbitrary
   core subsets.
5. A rank-one descendant of `558,412` slopes remains a genuine open branch.
6. No active-v4 owner or ledger atom is inferred.

## Finite controls

The primary verifier checks all rank-eleven descent dimensions, all
rank-twelve barrier dimensions, all `1,048,576` residual rank-one rows, the
527 formal weighted-line endpoint cells at the extremal row, and small
set-system controls for the dense-core inequality.  A separate implementation
replays the arithmetic with independent product formulas.
