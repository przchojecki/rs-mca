## Target

For the deployed KoalaBear row
\((p,n,K,m,w,B_*)=(2130706433,2097152,1048576,1116048,67472,
274980728111395087)\) over the sextic line field, start with one fixed

Counts are distinct affine slopes on one actual received line.  Pair labels,
passports, supports, gauges, and parallel graph edges are not slopes and may
not be substituted for them.  All selectors and minimizers are fixed once
before any cutoff scan.

## Acceptable adjacent outputs

- A weighted actual-record bipartite-core dichotomy with an exact minimum
  weighted-degree terminal.
- A conditional rank-eleven payment from a precisely quantified aggregate
  collision/common-core inequality.
- A sharp no-go theorem for every certificate using only local margins,
  cumulative pair-list caps, core deficiency, and per-pair ratio fibers.
- An exact reproduction of the #1167 rank-eleven wall and all proposed
  improvements with mutation-tested big integers.

## Evidence standard

A candidate theorem requires a complete derivation on actual records, source
pins to exact #1166/#1167 heads, exact arithmetic, and positive/negative
controls.  Provisional verification requires separate mathematics and
certificate reviews plus a blind reconstruction of the coupled incidence
step.  A handoff additionally requires a stable theorem, equality or sharpness
analysis, clean TeX, canonical certificate, hostile mutations, and an explicit
active-v4 chronology boundary.

## Prohibited overclaims

- Numerical agreement is not proof.
- An adjacent theorem is not evidence for the original conjecture without a proved implication.
- A minimum weighted multigraph degree is not distinct-neighbor expansion.
- A rank-eleven local payment is not active-v4 ledger movement or KoalaBear closure.
- No per-pair multiplicity improvement may contradict the exact parallel-star control.

## Stop and handoff conditions

Stop only at (i) exact rank-eleven payment, or (ii) a configuration-by-
configuration ceiling for the declared certificate class with the weakest
new correlation lemma frozen.  Before publication require an independent
mathematics reviewer and an independent certificate implementation.  Lean is
deferred until the coupled finite theorem stabilizes.
