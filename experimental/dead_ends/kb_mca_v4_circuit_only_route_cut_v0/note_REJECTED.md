# KoalaBear equality-wall circuit-only route cut

## 0. Verdict

The bounded-circuit reduction at the first equality wall proves that a
hypothetical \(69\)-class primitive packet produces at least \(60\) actual
minimal circuits of size \(3\) through \(10\), with no singleton carrier atom
and

\[
\operatorname{rank}(K_0|_{Z_C})\le 10-|C|.
\tag{0.1}
\]

Those conclusions are strong but not, by themselves, a cap-\(68\) theorem.
This note constructs an exact \(69\)-record countermodel satisfying the
deployed support cardinalities, affine-rank bound, pair-exchange floor,
canonical \(60\)-circuit conclusion, no-singleton property, and (0.1).

Consequently a proof using only those consequences cannot exclude the
\(69\)-record case or emit an active owner. The next theorem must consume
structure absent from this model: the split locator polynomials, the exact
source quotient and its degree bound, the primitive Hilbert--Burch line
module, or a same-record active-owner predicate.

This is a route cut for a weakened inference. It is not a counterexample to
the full primitive \(69\)-point theorem, does not construct a received line
or selector, and books no charge.

## 2. The rank-eight sign construction

For \(r\in\{0,\ldots,255\}\), let

\[
b_r=(b_{r,0},\ldots,b_{r,7})\in\{\pm1\}^8,
\qquad
b_{r,j}=(-1)^{\operatorname{bit}_j(r)}.
\tag{2.1}
\]

Take the \(69\) indices

\[
\mathcal R=\{0,1,\ldots,67,128\}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

For each sign type \(a\in\{\pm1\}^8\), introduce \(m_0\) carrier
coordinates and define

\[
F_r(a)=\langle b_r,a\rangle\in\mathbf F_p.
\tag{2.3}
\]

On the \(n_1\) common-one coordinates put \(F_r=1\) for every \(r\), and on
the \(n_0\) common-zero coordinates put \(F_r=0\) for every \(r\). Define

\[
Y_r=\{x\in V:F_r(x)\ne0\},
\qquad
Z_r=V\setminus Y_r.
\tag{2.4}
\]

For a fixed \(b_r\), the dot product in (2.3) is zero exactly when four of
the eight signs disagree. Hence every record has

\[
\binom84=70
\tag{2.5}
\]
zero sign types and \(186\) nonzero sign types. Therefore

\[
|Y_r|=186m_0+n_1=981{,}105=J,
\tag{2.6}
\]

\[
|Z_r|=70m_0+n_0=913{,}631=|V|-J.
\tag{2.7}
\]

All records belong to one affine translate of an eight-dimensional space:
the differences \(F_r-F_0\) are generated by the eight sign-coordinate
functions. The first independent star edges in the fixed index order are

\[
r=1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,
\tag{2.8}
\]

and have rank exactly eight over \(\mathbf F_p\).

## 3. Every pair clears the deployed exchange floor

Let \(d=d_H(b_r,b_s)\). Among the \(70\) zero types of \(F_r\), a type is
also zero for \(F_s\) precisely when \(d\) is even and exactly \(d/2\) of
the flipped coordinates carry a negative sign. Thus

\[
I(d)=
\begin{cases}
0,&d\ \text{odd},\\[2mm]
\displaystyle
\binom d{d/2}\binom{8-d}{4-d/2},&d\ \text{even}.
\end{cases}
\tag{3.1}
\]

The directed exchange is

\[
\Delta_{rs}=|Y_r\setminus Y_s|
=m_0\bigl(70-I(d)\bigr).
\tag{3.2}
\]

The selected indices contain no duplicate or antipodal pair. Across all
\(\binom{69}{2}=2{,}346\) pairs, the exact compressed histogram is

\[
\begin{array}{c|r}
\Delta_{rs}/m_0&\#\{r,s\}\\ \hline
30&575\\
34&581\\
70&1190.
\end{array}
\tag{3.3}
\]

In particular,

\[
\min_{r\ne s}\Delta_{rs}
=30\cdot5{,}274
=158{,}220
>67{,}472=c.
\tag{3.4}
\]

Moreover, \(F_r-F_s\) is nonzero on exactly the two exchange blocks and has
weight \(2\Delta_{rs}\). Thus even the exact exchange-block support property
of the canonical secants survives in this weakened model.

## 4. Sixty canonical minimal circuits

Root the star at \(r=0\) and use the basis (2.8). Every remaining selected
index \(r\) has Hamming weight \(h\ge2\), and

\[
F_r-F_0
=
\sum_{\operatorname{bit}_j(r)=1}
\bigl(F_{2^j}-F_0\bigr).
\tag{4.1}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
F_r
-\sum_{\operatorname{bit}_j(r)=1}F_{2^j}
+(h-1)F_0
=0.
\tag{4.2}
\]

The \(h\) basis secants are independent, so (4.2) is a minimal affine
circuit of size \(h+2\). There are exactly \(60\) nonbasis vertices, with

\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
|C|&4&5&6&7&8\\ \hline
\#C&17&21&15&6&1.
\end{array}
\tag{4.3}
\]

There are no affine three-circuits among the \(69\) sign vectors. Indeed, on
a coordinate where two distinct cube vertices disagree, a third point on
their affine line has sign \(1-2t\) times the first sign. Requiring another
\(\pm1\) value forces \(t=0\) or \(1\) in odd characteristic.

Every coefficient in (4.2) is nonzero and the coefficients sum to zero. If
one carrier coordinate belonged to exactly one support \(Y_i\) in the
circuit, evaluating (4.2) there would leave one nonzero term, a
contradiction. Hence every canonical circuit has no singleton carrier atom.

For these circuits there is no common zero among the \(256\) sign types:
\(F_0(a)=0\) and \(F_{2^j}(a)=0\) would imply
\(-2a_j=0\), impossible in odd characteristic. Therefore

\[
Z_C=\{\text{the }544{,}451\text{ common-zero coordinates}\},
\tag{4.4}
\]

and the restriction of the eight-dimensional difference space to \(Z_C\)
has rank zero. In particular, for every circuit in (4.3),

\[
\operatorname{rank}(K_0|_{Z_C})=0\le10-|C|.
\tag{4.5}
\]

Thus the complete bounded-circuit precursor survives with room to spare.
